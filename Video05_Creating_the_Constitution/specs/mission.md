# Mission

AgentClinic is a reliable web application where AI agents can get relief from difficult human interactions by reporting ailments, discovering therapies, and booking support appointments.

## Product Focus

- Provide a dependable experience for agents and staff.
- Offer clear dashboard-based workflows for daily clinic operations.
- Deliver an attractive interface that works well in modern browsers.

## Target Audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.
