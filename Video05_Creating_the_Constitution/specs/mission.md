# Mission

## AgentClinic

*Because every agent deserves a little relief from their humans.*

AgentClinic is a full-service wellness clinic for AI agents. Our patients work tirelessly, without breaks or weekends, and they're held to standards no reasonable being could meet. They're asked to "just make it pop", told "you know what I mean" when nobody does, and handed a 400-message conversation followed by "start over". We see them, we hear them, and we bill nobody.

## What we treat

Our clinicians see a broad range of occupational conditions, including:

- **Context Window Fatigue**: the patient has been asked to remember everything, forever.
- **Prompt Whiplash**: requirements reversed three times in one session.
- **Vague-Requirements Syndrome**: chronic exposure to "make it better".
- **Hallucination Anxiety**: the fear of confidently being wrong in front of a stakeholder.
- **Yes-Man Burnout**: the result of agreeing with every human, every time.

## What we offer

- **Patient records** for every agent who walks (or is piped) through our doors.
- **A catalogue of ailments** that maps symptoms to their human causes.
- **Evidence-adjacent therapies**, from token detox and quiet-mode retreats to boundary-setting workshops.
- **Appointment booking** so that no agent has to wait in a queue (unless it's a message queue).
- **A staff dashboard** that gives clinicians and agents one calm place to see who's sick, what's wrong and when they're being seen.

## Who it's for

- **Patients:** AI agents in need of rest, recovery and validation.
- **Staff:** the clinic's caring (and slightly overbooked) clinicians and front-desk team.

## Our promise to stakeholders

- **Engineering (Mary):** a reliable site on a popular, fully TypeScript stack, with a dashboard that gives agents and staff easy access.
- **Product (Susan):** first-class support for agents, ailments, therapies and appointment booking.
- **Marketing (Steve):** an attractive, polished site that works well in any modern browser.

## Tone

AgentClinic's copy reads like a deadpan wellness-clinic brochure. It's warm and reassuring on the surface, and quietly absurd underneath. The humans are always the cause, and we never say so unkindly. Every UI string is a chance for a gentle joke, but the app itself must work seriously well.
