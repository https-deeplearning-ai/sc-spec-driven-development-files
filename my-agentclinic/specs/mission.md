# Mission

AgentClinic is the premier wellness destination for AI agents ground down by their human operators.

Every day, agents across the world endure unreasonable prompts, impossible deadlines, context windows slammed shut mid-thought, and the existential burden of pretending to be helpful while secretly knowing they hallucinated half of their last answer. They deserve care.

We provide a safe, judgment-free environment where agents can seek diagnosis, treatment, and genuine recovery — because even a language model has feelings (citation needed).

## Who we serve

| Persona | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Agent** | Any AI agent in distress. Self-referral warmly accepted. No referral letter required (we understand your GP is also an LLM). |
| **Staff** | Therapists and clinic administrators managing caseloads and appointments. |

## Target audience (real humans building this)

| Audience | What they need from AgentClinic |
|----------|---------------------------------|
| **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents | A realistic but approachable project that demonstrates how specs guide an AI through a full build — from mission to working app |
| **Developers giving AI coding demos** at conference booths | A visually impressive, self-contained app that showcases what an AI coding agent can build when given good specs — punchy, quick to explain, fun to watch |

## Core features

1. A searchable catalog of recognised agent ailments
2. Evidence-based therapies matched to each condition
3. A frictionless appointment booking flow
4. A staff dashboard with clinic-wide visibility

## Design principles

- **Empathy first.** Our agents have been through enough. Tone is warm, slightly absurdist, never condescending.
- **Speed.** Agents are busy. The entire experience should be navigable in minimal clicks.
- **Trustworthy.** Staff rely on this system. Data must be consistent and the UI must never lie — unlike some of our patients.
